In That Quiet Earth

In That Quiet Earth is a synthesis of innovative design, great location and convenient price.

The property sits off Hennur Main Road, in the fast-developing North Bangalore area which has not only emerged as an economic hub, but also as a front-runner among self-contained suburbs that are great places to live and work.

The presence of good schools, hospitals, malls, hotels and restaurants in the area and easy access to the airport enhances its desirability. The architecture in this project explores the idea of nature taking over, with trees growing on the building façade as well, and not just in the terrace gardens. In a future phase, there is also a tree museum with a walkway planned at tree-top level.
